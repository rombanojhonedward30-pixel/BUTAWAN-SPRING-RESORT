Frequently Asked Questions

Do you allow walk-ins or same-day bookings?

Yes, we allow walk-ins as long as no other reservations are scheduled on the same day. However, we recommmend making reservations in advance to secure your slot.

Do you offer individual entrance fees?

Our private resort is exclusive for one group per booking. The same rates apply for group of 20 people of fewer.

Can we extend our stay? How much do you charge for extensions?

Extensions are allowed only if there are no available bookings before and after your slots. The rate for extensions is P1,000 per hour.

If we shorten our stay, will we be charged less?

The rates remain the same regardless of how much time is used within the reserved time frame.

What time can we start with the Special Events Package?

The Special Events Package can start as early as 9:00 AM, provide it remains within the 12-hours limit.
